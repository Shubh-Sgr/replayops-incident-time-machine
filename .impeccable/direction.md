# ReplayOps Direction Contract

## THESIS

ReplayOps is an industrial chronograph test bench translated into a production-incident workspace. It leads with a causal sequence rather than generic summary cards, giving time pressure a visible structure and keeping every hypothesis attached to evidence.

## OWN-WORLD

The world is a calibrated operations bench: cool blue-charcoal housings, pale instrument surfaces, incident orange controls, measured traces, stable record stamps, tabular readings, and restrained equipment depth. Calibration marks are functional; cross-hatching denotes conflicting evidence; blank space separates decisions from telemetry.

## STORY

Scan the active incident, scrub its causal sequence, inspect the selected evidence, search related records, ask a grounded assistant, and prepare a candidate replay. The assistant never replaces the record or the operator.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

The dashboard opens directly into an active incident trace. A calibrated event field shows propagation across services while ownership, severity, duration, and replay status stay on the same surface. Desktop uses a persistent operations rail; mobile compresses commands into a bottom dock and preserves a horizontally inspectable trace.
